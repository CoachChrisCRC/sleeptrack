How to Use

Set Your Profile & Target

Go to the Profile tab, fill out your age, stress, training intensity, and sleep latency habits. Click Save Profile & Calculate Target to let Coach Chris algorithm determine your baseline nightly sleep requirement.

Log Your Sleep Daily

On the Track tab, use the quick slider or expand the detailed Bed/Wake Time log—where you can input exact sleep latency in minutes to deduct wind-down delay from your time in bed.

Review Analytics & Trends

Check the Analytics tab to view your bar chart with live target/average displays, monthly recovery heatmap calendar, and deep-dive multi-timeframe coaching feedback.

Tap Info Icons for Guidance

Every card across the app features a subtle button in the header. Tap any of them anytime to learn what the metrics mean and how to act on them!

Recalculate Periodically

Life changes, and so does recovery. Circle back to update your profile stats every 4 to 6 weeks—or whenever your training volume or stress shifts significantly—so your sleep targets stay dialed in.
